# FareRight — Product Owner brief

*From: Priya (Product Owner) · "living document", last edited recently*

> ⚠️ Fictional scenario for training. Do not use real operational data.

## The vision

Passengers who are **overcharged** on contactless journeys should get their money back
**automatically** — no forms, no waiting. They should **see** refunds in the app and on the
website, and be able to **ask a question** if something looks wrong.

This is a **board priority**. We're demoing in **3 weeks**. Let's make it magical. ✨

## What "great" looks like

- Overcharges detected and refunded **automatically, within 24 hours**.
- A clear **"Refunds"** area in the app and website showing what was refunded and why.
- Passengers can **raise a query** on any refund.
- It "just works" — minimal support tickets.

## Scope (rough)

- Detect likely overcharges (incomplete journeys, reader outages, capping mistakes).
- Issue the refund back to the original card.
- Notify the passenger.
- Show refund history + a query button.

## Out of scope (I think?)

- Refunds for **season tickets / Travelcards** — that's a different team. *(Maybe? check with
  Ops.)*
- Cash / ticket-office refunds.

## Success metric

- 90% of eligible overcharges auto-refunded with no human involvement.
- Passenger satisfaction up.
